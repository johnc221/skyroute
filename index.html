What we do

Four things,
done properly

We are not a flight school. We are the team that gets you into one, gets you there, and makes sure the licence works when you come home.

01

Flight training placement

We negotiate your training block at Chino: a named syllabus, a fleet you have seen, and a target finish date agreed before you pay anyone.

02

DGCA licence conversion

Your hours, ratings and logbook format are mapped to the DGCA conversion checklist from day one — so you convert on schedule instead of discovering gaps after you land.

03

M-1 visa & I-20 support

I-20 issuance through the school, SEVIS, consulate paperwork, TSA vetting and fingerprinting — prepared with you, in the order the consulate expects.

04

Education loan assistance

Documentation for lenders who actually fund pilot training abroad: sanction letters, collateral guidance and disbursement timed to the school’s payment schedule.
