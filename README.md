# Skyroute Aviation

Flight training placement agency landing page — USA training placement, DGCA
licence conversion, M-1 visa/I-20 support, and education loan assistance for
Indian students training at Chino Airport, California.

Single self-contained `index.html` — no build step, no dependencies beyond
Google Fonts. Open it directly in a browser, or deploy as-is to GitHub Pages,
Netlify, Vercel, or any static host.

## Before going live

- **Counselling form**: connected to Formspree (`f/xqpkepyw`). Submit a test
  entry once and confirm the one-time email Formspree sends, or submissions
  won't deliver.
- **Photos**: ratings-board aircraft photos are Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA /
  CC BY), credited in the footer. The Chino hangar photo and any other
  supplied images should have their rights confirmed before launch.
- **Brand**: "Skyroute Aviation" is a placeholder name/logo — swap it (search
  for "Skyroute Aviation" in `index.html`) once you have the real brand.
- **Reviews**: the three student quotes are illustrative examples, clearly
  labeled as such on the page — replace with verified reviews before launch.
